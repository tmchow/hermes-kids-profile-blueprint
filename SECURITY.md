# Security and privacy reports

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/tmchow/hermes-kids-profile-blueprint/security/advisories/new) for a vulnerability or a safety or privacy concern that could put a child, family, account, or private profile at risk.

Do not post child or family personal information, credentials, transcripts, or private profiles in a public issue. Remove private details from descriptions, screenshots, logs, and examples before you submit a report.

Use a public issue for a documentation error or general improvement that does not expose private information or describe an exploitable weakness. Use private vulnerability reporting when disclosure could reveal a security weakness or private information, or could increase safety or privacy risk.
